About the Training & Placement Department

Anil Neerukonda Institute of Technology & Sciences (ANITS) has a dedicated Training & Placement Department committed to preparing students for successful careers through continuous training, skill development, industry interaction, and campus recruitment drives.

The department bridges the gap between academic learning and industry expectations by organizing recruitment drives, technical workshops, soft-skill training programs, aptitude coaching, mock interviews, resume-building sessions, and career guidance activities.

ANITS has consistently maintained an excellent placement record with leading multinational companies and core organizations visiting the campus every year. Students are provided with opportunities for both internships and full-time placements.


Department Objectives

  • Provide excellent placement opportunities.
  • Enhance technical and communication skills.
  • Develop industry-ready professionals.
  • Strengthen collaboration with companies.
  • Guide students towards successful careers.
